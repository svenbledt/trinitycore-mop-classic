# World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria Classic - Master Changelog

This document tracks the complete, detailed progression of MoP Classic from its Pre-Patch through Phase 5, including systemic overhauls, itemization, catch-up mechanics, and specific class tuning.

---

## 🌐 Global Baseline Adjustments (MoP Classic Architecture)

Rather than launching with original 5.0 balancing, MoP Classic utilizes a modified framework (5.5.x) to ensure smoother progression and better class balance from day one.

*   **PvE Content Scaling:** Heroic and Challenge Mode timers, boss health pools, and enrage windows were adjusted across all tiers to remain demanding despite modern class optimizations.
*   **PvP vs PvE Split Tuning:** MoP Classic fully utilizes the PvP-versus-PvE tuning split. Specific spells do different damage/healing values against players versus raid bosses.
*   **PvP Pacing:** Chain-CC duration and pressure were reduced globally. Defensive cooldowns were buffed for historically squishy specs.
*   **Faster Catch-up Windows:** Valor caps and gear ceilings were adjusted directly at launch to ensure re-rolls and alts stay viable across tiers.

## 🛡️ Pre-Patch 5.0.4: Attack on Theramore

The foundational systems update bridging Cataclysm to Mists of Pandaria.

### Systems & Mechanics
*   **Account-Wide Collections:** Mounts, pets, and achievements merged to account-wide status.
*   **Class Overhaul:** Legacy talent trees removed and replaced with the tier-based talent system (choices at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, and 90). Relic and Ranged weapon slots removed (Hunters equip bows in the main hand).
*   **Pet Battles:** Account-wide, turn-based companion pet dueling system unlocked.
*   **AoE Looting:** Introduced globally.

### Events & NPCs
*   **Theramore's Fall (Scenario):** First look at the new 3-player Scenario system.
*   **Key NPCs:** Ji Firepaw & Aysa Cloudsinger (Wandering Isle), Jaina Proudmoore, Garrosh Hellscream.

### Items & Catch-up
*   **Event Loot:** Provided **ilvl 384** catch-up gear for Cataclysm characters.
*   **Toys/Cosmetics:** *Mini Mana Bomb* (Horde), *Theramore Tabard* (Alliance).

## 🐼 Phase 1 (Patch 5.0.5): Mists of Pandaria Launch

The official release of the expansion, opening Pandaria and the first raid tier.

### World & NPCs
*   **Key NPCs:** Lorewalker Cho, Wrathion the Black Prince, The August Celestials.
*   **World Bosses:** Sha of Anger (Kun-Lai Summit) and Galleon (Valley of the Four Winds).
*   **The Farm:** Sunsong Ranch (Tillers) opens, allowing players to grow crafting materials (ore, herbs, leather) daily.

### Raids, Dungeons & Itemization (Tier 14)
*   **Dungeons (Normal/Heroic):** Normal drops **ilvl 450**; Heroics drop **ilvl 463**.
*   **Challenge Modes:** Gear normalized to ilvl 463. Rewards cosmetic class armor and *Pandaren Phoenix* mounts.
*   **Raids (Mogu'shan Vaults, Heart of Fear, Terrace of Endless Spring):**
    *   LFR: **ilvl 476 / 483**
    *   Normal: **ilvl 489 / 496**
    *   Heroic: **ilvl 502 / 509**
*   **Notable Drops:** *Reins of the Astral Cloud Serpent* (Elegon), *Claws of Shek'zeer* (Heart of Fear).

### Currencies & Catch-up
*   **Justice Points:** Used for **ilvl 458** Rare gear.
*   **Valor Points:** Capped at 1000/week, used for **ilvl 489** Epic reputation gear.
*   **Elder Charms of Good Fortune:** First bonus roll currency introduced.

### Legendary Questline: Chapter 1
*   **Items:** *Sigil of Power* & *Sigil of Wisdom*.
*   **Reward:** ilvl 500 Legendary Gem (e.g., *Crystallized Dread*) for Sha-Touched Weapons.

## ⚔️ Phase 2 (Patch 5.1): Landfall

The Alliance and Horde fleets arrive in Krasarang Wilds. This patch introduced major class balancing based on Phase 1 data.

### World & NPCs
*   **Faction Hubs:** Lion's Landing (King Varian Wrynn) and Domination Point (Garrosh Hellscream).
*   **Brawler's Guild:** Underground solo-PvE arena opens (NPCs: Bizmo / Boss Bazzelflange). Reaching Rank 8 awards powerful Heirloom fist weapons (*Brawler's Razor Claws*).

### Class Stat Balancing & Tuning (Implemented mid-phase)
*   **Druid:** Balance Nature/Arcane damage bonus increased to 25%. Feral Savage Roar buffed to 45%; Predatory Swiftness increased to 12s. Guardian Bear Form damage buffed by 15%.
*   **Mage:** Arcane Missiles/Blast/Barrage damage heavily buffed (15-29%). Fire Pyroblast damage increased by 30%.
*   **Monk:** Windwalker Blackout Kick damage increased by 25%. Mistweaver Enveloping Mists healing increased by 48%; Eminence healing buffed to 42% of damage dealt.
*   **Paladin:** Holy Crusader Strike mana cost reduced; Eternal Flame periodic healing reduced by 28%. Protection Sacred Shield buffed by 43%. Retribution core rotational abilities damage increased by 10%; Templar's Verdict by 20%.
*   **Shaman/Warlock:** Elemental Lightning Bolt damage increase buffed to 20%. Affliction Corruption damage increase nerfed to 20%; Soul Swap snapshotting removed. Demonology Wild Imp damage increased by 60%.

### Map, PvP & System Changes
*   **Map Fixes:** Caverns of Time mini-map art corrected. "Shu, the Spirit of Water" POI markers fixed.
*   **PvP Catch-Up:** Earning 4000 Conquest on one character grants a 50% Conquest gain buff to all alts. Vicious Gladiator gear added to Gadgetzan vendor. Mid-season epic PvP gear upgrades disabled.
*   **Arena Titles:** Ladder split; achievements and titles became bracket-specific (e.g., *Gladiator (3v3)*).
*   **Leveling:** Level 85-90 XP bonus increased massively from 10% to 65%.

### Itemization & Catch-up
*   **Item Upgrades:** Ethereals added. Players can upgrade Epic items by +4 ilvl twice using Valor, and Rares using Justice.
*   **Faction Catch-up:** New hub reputations offer **ilvl 496** gear.
*   **Grand Commendations:** Reaching Revered allows purchasing a token that doubles rep gains for all alts.

### Legendary Questline: Chapter 2
*   **Reward:** *Eye of the Black Prince* (Adds a Prismatic Socket to Phase 1/2 weapons). Drop rates for Chapter 1 Sigils massively increased.

## ⚡ Phase 3 (Patch 5.2): The Thunder King

The Isle of Thunder unlocks, bringing a massive raid and new item mechanics.

### World & NPCs
*   **Key NPCs:** Emperor Lei Shen, Ra-den (Heroic-only hidden boss).
*   **World Bosses:** Oondasta (Isle of Giants), Nalak (Isle of Thunder). Drop ilvl 522 PvE/PvP gear.

### Raids & Itemization (Tier 15)
*   **Throne of Thunder:**
    *   LFR: **ilvl 502**
    *   Normal: **ilvl 522**
    *   Heroic: **ilvl 535**
*   **Thunderforged Gear:** Normal/Heroic gear can drop with a random **+6 ilvl** bonus (e.g., Normal Thunderforged is 528).
*   **Notable Drops:** *Unerring Vision of Lei-Shen*, *Rune of Re-Origination*, *Reins of the Cobalt Primordial Direhorn*.

### Catch-up Mechanics
*   **Shado-Pan Assault:** Raid-tied faction allowing non-raiders to buy **ilvl 522** gear with Valor.
*   **Troves of the Thunder King:** Solo treasure-room scenario for gold and catch-up tokens.
*   **Currencies:** Mogu Runes of Fate (Bonus Rolls). Giant Dinosaur Bones (Isle of Giants mount farming).

### Legendary Questline: Chapter 3
*   **Items:** *Secrets of the Empire* & *Titan Runestones*.
*   **Reward:** *Crown of Heaven* (Legendary Meta Gems with massive procs, e.g., Capacitive Primal Diamond).

## 🔥 Phase 4 (Patch 5.3): Escalation

A narrative bridge patch focusing on the Darkspear Rebellion.

### World & Catch-up Mechanics
*   **Battlefield: Barrens:** Open-world event against Kor'kron Commanders. Collect resources for *Latent Kor'kron Armor*, which creates **ilvl 489** alt gear.
*   **Heroic Scenarios:** Scaled-up 3-player content. Awards a cache with a chance at **ilvl 516** gear (the primary 5-man catch-up for this era).
*   **PvP Changes:** Base resilience set to 65% for all players. PvP gear ilvl normalized in instanced PvP to bridge the gap between fresh and geared players.

### Legendary Questline: Chapter 4
*   **Reward:** Epic **ilvl 600** Cloaks. Catch-up drop rates for prior legendary items heavily increased.

## 🏔️ Phase 5 (Patch 5.4): Siege of Orgrimmar

The climatic final phase, introducing the ultimate alt-gearing zone.

### World & Catch-up (The Timeless Isle)
*   **The Timeless Isle:** The definitive catch-up zone.
*   **Timeless Armor Tokens:** BoA tokens dropping from chests/rares. Create **ilvl 496** gear for the user's spec.
*   **Burden of Eternity:** Consumable applied to Timeless Tokens to upgrade the resulting gear to **ilvl 535** (Normal raid equivalent).
*   **World Bosses:** Four August Celestials drop LFR Tier 16 (**ilvl 528**). Ordos drops **ilvl 559** gear.
*   **Server Mergers:** Virtual PvE and PvP clusters implemented to stabilize realm populations and economies.

### Raids & Itemization (Tier 16)
*   **Siege of Orgrimmar:**
    *   LFR: **ilvl 528**
    *   Flexible Raid (NEW 10-25 scaling): **ilvl 540**
    *   Normal: **ilvl 553**
    *   Heroic: **ilvl 566**
*   **Warforged Gear:** Replaces Thunderforged, granting a +6 ilvl boost.
*   **Heirlooms:** Garrosh drops BoA weapons (ilvl 566-582) that scale from level 90 to 100.
*   **Notable Drops:** *Kor'kron Juggernaut* (Mount), *Tusks of Mannoroth* (Cosmetic shoulders).

### Legendary Questline: The Finale
*   **Reward:** The **ilvl 608** Legendary Cloaks (e.g., *Fen-Yu, Fury of Xuen*, *Xing-Ho, Breath of Yu'lon*). These feature build-defining visual and mechanical procs.
